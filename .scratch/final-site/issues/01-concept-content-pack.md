# 01: Prepare the shared concept content pack

**What to build:** A review-safe content and structure pack that all three visual concepts use, including the five-page model, agreed homepage sections, labelled placeholders and provenance boundaries.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The pack expresses the approved Home, Café, Restaurant, About and Visit structure and primary journeys.
- [ ] Unresolved facts and imagery are represented by unmistakable review placeholders rather than plausible invented content.
- [ ] Exact phone, prices, hours and booking method are not required unless they materially affect a concept decision.
- [ ] The pack is reviewed against the specification, glossary and content-status record.
