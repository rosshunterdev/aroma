1Header: wordmark, Menu, About, Visit

The wordmark links home, so the separate HOME link goes. MENU joins the header, and Contact is renamed Visit. About stays hidden until the client sends their story.

Fixes: Menu isn't in the nav, ABOUT 404s, two links both go home.

2Hero: who Aroma is and how to get there

The page's single h1 sits here: the client's heritage line. The three facts visitors need most sit right under it, and Call is the main action.

Fixes: no h1, and nothing above the fold says what kind of cafe this is.

3Your tabs, kept, with no autoplay

Food, Coffee and Tea stay, as decided in 008. They change only on click or arrow keys. Egg Coffee moves to Coffee and Matcha to Tea. Try them in the mock.

Fixes: WCAG 2.2.2 (auto-rotation), the broken tabs pattern, Egg Coffee sitting under Tea.

4The button follows the selected tab

"See all coffee" opens menu.html#coffee. The menu page uses the same three sections, so the home tabs work as a preview of it.

Fixes: home and menu categories don't match.

5Visit is a real section

It sits in the normal page flow, not pinned to a corner, so it can't overlap other content on mobile. Hours show am and pm.

Fixes: the absolutely positioned Visit block, and "7–3" being ambiguous.

6Footer repeats the essentials

Address, phone, email and socials, so anyone who scrolls to the bottom finds them.

All contact details are provisional until the client confirms them.

Site map: current vs proposed

Same structure on a phone

  • Photos move above the text, and the three columns stack.
  • The fact pills are at least 44px tall, so they're easy to tap. Call is the filled one, within thumb reach after the photo.
  • Visit is in the normal flow, so nothing overlaps.
  • The header wraps under the wordmark. At this width it still fits three links, which is another reason to drop HOME.
