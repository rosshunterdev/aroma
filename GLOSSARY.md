# Aroma product glossary

Use these terms consistently in discovery, specifications and customer-journey discussions. Definitions describe the product model only; they do not assert unconfirmed operating facts.

## Café

One of Aroma's two distinct customer-facing offerings within one shared brand. It covers the established, relatively casual and coffee-oriented experience. Its current menu, hours and contact details still require client confirmation.

Avoid using **day offering** as though daytime hours are confirmed.

## Restaurant

The second customer-facing offering Aroma intends to introduce alongside the Café within the same recognisable brand. Its expression may feel more evening, refined, professional and considered, but should not resemble a separate business. A specifically romantic mood is not required. Its name, menu, operating days/hours, launch date and booking method are not yet confirmed.

Avoid using **evening offering** as though evening service details are confirmed.

## Vietnamese highlight

A small red accent or label applied to specific client-approved special items. It is a restrained marker within the wider minimalist Kiwi-oriented brand direction, not a separate ornamental theme. Which items qualify and how they should be described require client approval.

Avoid treating all Vietnamese dishes, Aroma's full identity or the entire interface as a Vietnamese highlight.

## Kiwi-oriented

An atmospheric direction for Aroma: unpretentious, approachable and grounded in local-neighbourhood hospitality. It does not mean literal New Zealand motifs, symbols or themed decoration.
